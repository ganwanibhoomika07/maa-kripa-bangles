About Maa Kripa Bangles

Maa Kripa started with a simple wish: a bangle should feel special every time you put it on. Our artisans in Rajasthan shape lac, glass and gold-plated brass by hand, so each piece has its own weight and shine.

We work directly with family workshops, pay fair prices, and polish and pack every set ourselves.
